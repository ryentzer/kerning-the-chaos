# Studies

Small pieces made to learn one thing, the way a painter makes a study of a hand before the portrait. Each study gets its own folder named `YYYY-MM-DD-short-name/` with an `index.html` and a few lines in a `README.md` on what it trained and what was learned.

Ideas come from the practice plans at the end of each guide: chapter 13 of Motion & Matter and chapter 12 of Anatomy of a Reveal.
